Microphones and headphones

Purandara has to hear your voice and not its own harmonium. Here is how each setup works.

Headphones (best)

Wired or Bluetooth headphones keep the app’s sound out of the microphone completely. The phone or tablet’s own microphone hears your voice; keep it within a metre or so of you.

The built-in speaker

With echo cancellation on, the app removes its own sound from what the microphone hears. It works well; the app shows “Headphones give the most accurate results” as a reminder.

  • Stay quiet during the count-in, so the app can measure how much of its sound reaches the microphone.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the system lowers the playback volume while echo cancellation is working. Turn the volume up, or use headphones.

Bluetooth microphones and neckbands

A Bluetooth microphone can only be used in “call” mode, which also sends the app’s sound to the Bluetooth device at phone-call quality. Neither Android nor iOS can record from a Bluetooth microphone while playing through the device’s speaker. Use the Bluetooth device as headphones instead, and let the phone or tablet’s microphone hear you.

A USB microphone

A USB microphone close to you and away from the speaker hears your voice clearly. Turn off Echo cancellation in practice settings to keep the volume steady; the count-in still measures how much of the app’s sound reaches the microphone.

Changing headphones or speakers

On Android, iPhone and iPad, a change of audio output stops playback, the tanpura and listening. Nothing restarts automatically. Tap Play, or Start again in Shruti practice or Find my Sa, when you are ready; the next start uses the output selected in the system. Losing the microphone, for example when a Bluetooth headset switches off, has the same effect.

On Android, connecting or disconnecting an audio device also conservatively stops a listening-only or background session. Not every output selection can be detected when nothing is playing. A change that stops the session also cancels its normal fresh start when you return to the app.

If it says “Not hearing you yet”

  • Check that microphone access is allowed for Purandara in the system settings.
  • Sing a little louder, or move the microphone closer.
  • Make sure you stayed quiet during the count-in. If not, play the exercise again.
  • On the speaker, keep echo cancellation on.
