Find my Sa

Not sure which Sa to use? Sing two notes and Purandara suggests one.

How it works

  1. On the home screen, tap Find my Sa.
  2. Sing your lowest comfortable note and hold it for a couple of seconds. Comfortable means easy and clear, not the lowest growl you can reach.
  3. Then sing your highest comfortable note and hold it. It needs to be clearly higher, at least four notes above the first.
  4. Read the suggestion, then tap Use this to set it as your Sa, or Try again.

How the Sa is chosen

Varisais go a little below Sa and a little above upper Sa. The suggestion leaves room for both: lower Pa no lower than your lowest note, and upper Ri no higher than your highest, in the middle of the Sas that allow it. If your range is too narrow for both, you get the closest fit, and the app says so.

Your range grows with practice. Try again in a few weeks; a teacher’s advice comes first.
