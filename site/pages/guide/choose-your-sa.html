Choose your Sa

Your Sa, or shruthi, is the home note everything else is sung against. Purandara uses it for the harmonium, the tanpura and the marking of your notes.

Pick it from the list

Tap the Sa on the home screen, or Sa in the player’s practice settings. Each choice shows its keyboard note and kattai, for example “D3 · 2 kattai”. The list is grouped by the voices that usually sing there:

GroupSa
Lower voicesG2 to B2 (5 to 7 kattai, lower octave)
Most menC3 to E3 (1 to 3 kattai)
Most womenF3 to A♯3 (4 to 6½ kattai)
Higher voicesB3 to C4 (7 to 1 kattai, upper octave)

The groups are a starting point, not a rule. If your teacher has given you a Sa, use theirs. If you are unsure, Find my Sa suggests one from your voice.

Fine-tune it

Teachers’ shruti boxes and recordings are not always exactly on a keyboard note. Fine tune moves your Sa up or down by up to 50 cents, one cent at a time. A fine-tuned Sa shows the difference, such as “C3 · 1 kattai +12¢”.

  • Android: in the player’s practice settings, under Sa.
  • iPhone and iPad: at the top of the Sa list.

The fine-tune applies everywhere: the harmonium, the tanpura and the marking of your notes.

Check it with the tanpura

On the home screen, tap Play tanpura. The tanpura plays pa SA SA sa at your Sa until you tap Stop tanpura or open an exercise. If singing Sa over it feels strained, try a lower Sa; if it feels weak or breathy, try a higher one.
