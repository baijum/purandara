Play a varisai

Open any exercise from the home screen to play it on the harmonium, over the tanpura, in Adi tala.

Reading the screen

The tala strip
The eight beats of Adi tala: x clap, 1 2 3 finger counts, x clap, v wave, x clap, v wave. The beat being heard is lit.
The notation
One row per avarta (tala cycle). A single bar ends a group of the tala, a double bar ends the avarta. A comma holds the swara before it for one more beat: keep singing through it. S′ is the upper Sa. The swara being heard is highlighted.
The line below the notation
While playing, it shows your Sa, “Count-in…” during the count-in, and “Pass 2 of 4” when an exercise repeats to fill whole cycles at a faster speed.

Controls

Play and Stop
Play starts from the beginning, with one cycle of tala clicks first if count-in is on. Stop fades out.
Tempo
Beats per minute of the tala, in steps of 5. Start slow: 50 to 60 is plenty for a new exercise.
Speed
Kala 1 to 4: one, two, three or four swaras per beat, at the same tempo.
Microphone
Turns sing-along on or off.
Practice settings
The sliders button: Sa, tuning, harmonium guide, drone and volumes, click, count-in, repeat, echo cancellation and strictness. See Practice settings.

Changing a setting while an exercise plays restarts it with the new setting.

If the output changes

On both platforms, changing headphones or speakers stops playback, the tanpura and sing-along listening, with no automatic restart. Tap Play when you are ready; the next start uses the output selected in the system.

The app stops playing when it leaves the screen, for example when you switch apps or lock the phone. An output change while backgrounded also cancels any normal fresh-session resume on return.
