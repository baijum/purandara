Credits

Purandara is built on generous work by others. Thank you.

In the app

Harmonium
Recorded by donyaquick in the Euterpea Studio at Yale University, and cut into single notes by cabled_mess, on Freesound (CC0). Pitch-corrected for the app.
Tanpura
“Acoustic Tanpura 10” by Sankalp Gulati, recorded at Dhrupad Sansar, IIT Bombay (CC BY 4.0), cut into seamless loops and pitch-shifted for the app.
Pitch model
SwiftF0 by lars76 (MIT), run with ONNX Runtime by Microsoft (MIT).
Exercises
The traditional varisai sequences of the Carnatic teaching tradition that began with Purandara Dasa.
Alankarams, gitams and swarajatis
Notation and words from karnatik.com by Rani, checked against Shivkumar Kalyanaraman’s archive, veenagana.org and the book Sangeeta Bala Padam, Part 1 (Giri Trading Agency). The gitams are by Purandara Dasa; the swarajati Sambasiva Yenave is by Chinnakrishna Dasar.

The full list, with licences and what was changed, is in ATTRIBUTION.md.
