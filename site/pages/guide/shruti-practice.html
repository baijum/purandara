Shruti practice

A warm-up before the varisais: the tanpura plays, and you see exactly where your voice is.

Open it

On the home screen, in the Your Sa (Shruthi) card, tap Practise Sa. The tanpura starts at your Sa. For the first two seconds stay quiet while the app listens to the room, then sing.

Your pitch

The last ten seconds of your voice are drawn against lines for each swara, from lower Pa to upper Sa, with Sa marked. The reading names what you are singing, for example “S · in tune · +3¢ · C3”. A dot means the lower octave and ′ the upper.

Hold a note

Choose a note, Sa, Pa or upper Sa, and a goal of 10, 20 or 30 seconds. On iPhone and iPad, tap Change beside “Hold Sa for 10 seconds”; on Android the choices are on the screen.

Then sing the note and hold it. The bar and the text show how long you have held it, and your best so far. When a hold reaches the goal you see “Goal reached”, which screen readers announce.

  • The note counts while it is close or in tune at your strictness, in the right octave.
  • A quick breath or wobble, up to about a third of a second, doesn’t end a hold.

If the output changes

On both platforms, changing headphones or speakers stops the tanpura and listening, with no automatic restart. Tap Start again to use the system-selected output and do a fresh room check. Returning from the background normally starts a fresh session, but not if an output change cancelled it.

Without a microphone

If you haven’t allowed the microphone, the tanpura still plays and the screen offers Allow microphone.
