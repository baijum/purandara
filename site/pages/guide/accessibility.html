Accessibility

Purandara is built to be used with a screen reader and with large text. If something doesn’t work for you, please report it; it is treated as seriously as any other bug.

Screen readers

  • Notation: each avarta is read as one sentence, for example “Avarta 1: S R G M, P hold, S R”, where “hold” is a comma.
  • The tala strip: read as the pattern, “Adi tala: clap 1 2 3, clap wave, clap wave”.
  • Sing-along: the pitch lane is read as your current reading, such as “P, in tune, plus 3 cents, G3”, and marks are read with their notes. The summary after each pass is announced once, never while the music plays.
  • Shruti practice: your pitch is read the same way, and “Goal reached” is announced.
  • Find my Sa: the suggestion is announced when it is ready.
  • Every button has a label, and controls such as tempo say their value.

Large text

All text follows the system text size. At the largest sizes, drawings such as the pitch lane and the melodic shapes give their space to text, and nothing is cut off.

Reduced motion

Purandara honours the system’s reduce-motion setting.
