Privacy

Purandara collects nothing. It works entirely on your phone or tablet.

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No internet. The app doesn’t connect to anything; every feature works in airplane mode.
  • No tracking, analytics or ads.
  • Your voice stays on your device. The microphone is used only while sing-along, Shruti practice or Find my Sa is listening. Sound is analysed as it arrives and never recorded or stored.
  • What is stored, on your device only: your practice settings, and for each exercise when you last practised it, how many times, and your best sing-along result. Uninstalling the app removes them.
